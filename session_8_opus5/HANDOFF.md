# Handoff — the single smallest remaining proof obligation

Everything else in the averaged programme (Proposition 3 of `THEOREM_AND_PROOF.md`) is
either proved or clears its union bound with a large margin. One statement does not.

---

## The obligation

> **(Q) Neighbourhood quasirandomness of the exceptional set.**
>
> Let `E(i) := { u ∈ V(i) : d_2(u,i) > s_2(t_i) + tol(t_i) }` be the exceptional vertices at
> time `i` (nonempty by Theorem 2 — that is the point). Show that for every `v ∈ V(i)` and
> every `l ∈ {2,3}`,
> ```
>    Sigma_l(v,i) := sum_{e ∈ d_l(v)} sum_{u ∈ e \ {v}}  d_2(u,i)
>                 <= (l-1) d_l(v) · ( s_2(t_i) + tol(t_i) ),
> ```
> i.e. that `v`'s neighbours in the evolving structure do not over-sample `E(i)`.

## Why it is not free

`E(i)` is not an arbitrary small set. By Lemma 1(b) a vertex `u` lands in `E(i)` exactly
when several of the chosen vertices `y_j` lie on a **short-primitive-direction line through
`u`** — in the extreme case, `u`'s own row or column. The vertices `w` that are `d_2`-joined
to `v` are, by the same lemma, produced by the *same* lines: `w` is joined to `v` when some
chosen `y_j` sits on a line whose perpendicular bisector with `v` passes through `w`.

So the set being averaged over and the set of large values are generated by one common
mechanism. A first-moment estimate gives the right answer with room —
```
   E[ Sigma_l(v) ] - (l-1) d_l(v) s_2  =  O( d_l(v) · n / sqrt(log n) )
                                       vs. permitted  Theta( d_l(v) · sigma n sqrt(log n) ),
```
a margin of `Theta(sigma log n)` — but the first moment does not see the correlation, and
**nothing proved in this session determines its sign**. If `v`'s neighbourhood is biased
*towards* the same lines, the margin can be eaten.

## What a solution looks like

Either of the following would close it:

1. **A positive-correlation-free decomposition.** Show that `Sigma_l(v)` splits as
   (contribution of lines through `v`) + (contribution of lines not through `v`), that the
   second is genuinely averaged, and that the first is `O(n·polylog)` deterministically —
   because only `O(1)` short primitive directions pass through any fixed point at each scale.
   The counting in Lemma 1(c) is the natural tool: for scale `s` there are `Theta(s)`
   primitive directions and `Theta(n/s)` points per line, so a single vertex sits on
   `Theta(s)` lines of scale `s` and the total over `s <= K` is `Theta(K^2)`, versus
   `Theta(nK)` vertices reachable. That ratio, `K/n`, is what has to be shown to be the
   right measure of the bias.

2. **A negative-association argument.** Show `{J_u}_{u ∈ V}` (the exceptional-step counts)
   are negatively associated conditional on the chosen sequence, which would both close the
   Chernoff step of Proposition 3(3b) (currently marked CONDITIONAL for the same reason) and
   give (Q) directly.

## Also needed, but smaller

- **(H-surv)**, the survival hypothesis of Theorem 2 (`CLAIM_REGISTRY` B1): a fixed
  `Theta(n)`-element set, and a fixed grid line, each retain a constant fraction of their
  vertices while a constant fraction of the grid survives. Measured ratio `Theta(1)`. This
  should follow from BB's own condition (P) plus a second-moment argument; it was not done.
- The independence audit in Proposition 3(3b), which (Q) route 2 would subsume.

## What must not be redone

- Do **not** revisit "average `Delta_2` or `Gamma`". At `r = 3` both are initial conditions
  for monotone quantities; there is nothing dynamic there (ATTACK_LOG A1, registry S1/S2/S5).
- Do **not** attempt to rescue the pointwise `d_2` condition with a sharper concentration
  inequality, a different `zeta`/`delta`, a longer or shorter horizon, self-correcting error
  functions, or sparsification of the ground set. Theorem 2 bounds the probability of the bad
  event **from below**, so it applies to all of these (ATTACK_LOG A5).
- Do **not** infer asymptotics from the finite-`n` tables. They are sanity checks on proved
  statements and on (H-surv); every asymptotic claim in this session has a proof or is marked
  CONDITIONAL/OPEN.
